## Why this matters now

US utility patents must be renewed with maintenance fees at 3.5, 7.5 and 11.5 years after grant (35 U.S.C. 41(b)). Each fee has a 6-month grace period with a surcharge. If a fee is still unpaid at the end of the grace period, the patent expires. It can be reinstated if the delay is shown to be unintentional (37 CFR 1.378; MPEP 2590), and anyone who began using the invention in good faith while it was expired may keep some rights to continue (35 U.S.C. 41(c)).
