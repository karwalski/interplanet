## What the study is about

We are studying how small teams communicate by text when messages take several
minutes to arrive, as they would between a spacecraft and mission control. You
will work in a team of three: two people play crew members and one plays a
flight controller. Together you will diagnose simulated faults in a fictional
spacecraft life-support system.

## What you will be asked to do

- Take part in a briefing and, depending on your group, a short training
  session on message structure. [duration]
- Take part in [number] sessions of about [duration] each, using a text chat
  tool. Messages between crew and flight controller will be delayed. At times
  messages may be held or, in some sessions, lost.
- After each session, complete a workload questionnaire (NASA-TLX) and
  [other questionnaires].

Total time: [total]. Compensation: [amount and form, or none].

## Possible risks and discomforts

time. [Any other risks identified by the committee.]
